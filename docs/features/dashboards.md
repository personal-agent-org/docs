# Dashboards, entities & scenes

If you connect smart-home and productivity [integrations](integrations.md), Personal
Agent gives you a full Home-Assistant-style layer: **entities** with live state,
**dashboards** to see and control them, **scenes** to snapshot states, and a **logbook**
of what changed.

## Entities

An **entity** is a single thing with state — a light, a sensor, a project, a calendar.
Integrations create them automatically; you can also create your own. They live on the
[Knowledge](memory.md) page (the **Entities** view), where each shows its state, whether
it's **RAG-indexed** for search, and when it last synced.

- **Helpers** (*Helfer*) — create your own simple entities: a **toggle**, a **number**
  (slider), a **button/counter**, a **select** (dropdown), **text** or a
  **date/time**.
- **Areas & floors** (*Bereiche & Etagen*) — organize entities by room and floor.
- **Sync now** (*Synchronisieren*) pulls the latest state from an integration.

Entities are **controllable**: a dashboard card, a [workflow](workflows.md) and the
assistant all drive them through one pipeline, and changes appear live.

## Dashboards

**Dashboards** (*Dashboards*) are Lovelace-style boards of cards over your entities,
agenda and more. Each dashboard appears as **its own entry in the sidebar**.

### Managing dashboards

The **Dashboards** page is where you **create, rename, re-icon and delete** boards, set
one as the **default on this device**, and choose whether each shows in the sidebar. When
you create one, you choose how it starts:

- **Empty** (*Leeres Dashboard*),
- **Generated from your entities** (by domain), or
- **Generated by areas**.

Auto-generated dashboards stay in sync until you **take control** (*Übernehmen*), which
freezes them so you can edit freely.

### Editing a dashboard

Open a dashboard and click **Edit** (*Bearbeiten*). You get:

- **Views** (*Ansichten*) — tabbed pages, each laid out as **Sections** (titled columns)
  or a free **Grid**, with view types like sections, masonry, panel and sidebar.
- **Cards** — added from a searchable **card picker** with live previews, then arranged
  by drag-and-resize. Cut/copy/paste, duplicate and move cards between views.
- **Badges** (*Badges*) — compact status chips at the top of a view.
- **Conditions** (*Bedingungen*) — show a card or section only when a condition holds
  (state, numeric state, screen size, time, and/or/not).
- **GUI or YAML** — edit each card visually or as YAML, and there's a raw-config editor
  for the whole board. **Undo/redo** throughout.

### Card types

There's a wide card set, including:

- **Read-outs** — entity, glance, sensor, gauge, statistic, history, statistics graph,
  logbook, calendar, agenda, weather forecast, map, markdown, picture, iframe, clock.
- **Controls** — tile, toggle, button, light, thermostat, humidifier, select, number,
  counter, scene, media control, alarm panel, to-do list.
- **Picture & registry** — picture-elements, picture-glance, picture-entity, area,
  device.
- **Layout** — grid, stack (horizontal/vertical), heading, conditional and entity-filter.

Custom [chat modes](chat-controls.md#chat-modes) can pin a dashboard's cards into the
chat's side panel, so the controls you need sit right next to the conversation.

## Scenes

A **scene** (*Szene*) captures the current state of a set of entities so you can re-apply
it later with one click. On the **Scenes** page (*Szenen*, in the account menu):
**New scene** (*Neue Szene*), name it, pick the entities to capture, and later
**Activate** (*Aktivieren*) to restore them all at once.

## Logbook

The **Logbook** (*Logbuch*, in the account menu) is a reverse-chronological feed of
recent **entity state changes** — what changed, from which state to which, and when. Filter
it by integration to focus on one domain.
