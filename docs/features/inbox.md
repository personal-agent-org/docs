# Inbox & contacts

Connect your messaging channels and Personal Agent becomes a single place to stay on top
of everything coming in — triaged, summarized, and with replies drafted for you to
approve.

## The Inbox

The **Inbox** (*Posteingang*, in the sidebar) gathers messages from every channel you've
connected — **email, Signal, WhatsApp, Matrix, Zulip**. As each message arrives, the
agent **triages** it: works out what it is, summarizes it, and — when a reply is wanted —
drafts one.

The sidebar badge shows how many items are **open**.

### Working the inbox

- **Filter** by status — **Open** (*Offen*), **Answered**, **Seen**, **All**,
  **Starred**, **Snoozed** — and by **channel**, plus a search box.
- Each message carries a **status**: **New**, **Needs reply** (*Antwort offen*),
  **Answered** or **Seen**.
- **Star** (*Markieren*) anything to come back to it, or **Snooze** (*Zurückstellen*) it
  to reappear later — in 3 hours, this evening, tomorrow morning or next week.
- Mark items **Seen** or **Done**, or select several at once for bulk actions.

### Drafts you approve

This is the heart of it: you don't write routine replies, you **approve** them. For a
message that needs an answer, the agent shows a **draft reply** (*Antwort-Entwurf*) with
the recipient and the proposed text. You can:

- **Approve & send** (*Freigeben & senden*) as-is,
- **Edit** (*Bearbeiten*) the text first, or
- **Reject** (*Verwerfen*) it.

You can also ask for a **suggestion** (*Vorschlagen*) on demand, **forward**, or
**reply to all**. Nothing is ever sent without your go-ahead.

### Keeping the noise out

Two profile settings shape what reaches you (under **Settings → Profile**):

- **Priorities** (*Prioritäten*) — what matters to you (investors, key customers,
  family…), which helps the agent judge importance.
- **Inbox rules** (*Posteingang-Regeln*) and exact **drop patterns** — what should
  **never** appear (newsletters, automated notifications…). Matching messages are
  dropped before triage, so they're cheap and silent.

You can turn triage on or off per account with the **Inbox triage** toggle on the
integration (**Settings → Integrations**) — messages still sync, they just won't be
triaged.

## Contacts

Behind every message is a person. The **Contacts** page (*Kontakte*, in the sidebar) is a
**person-centric, cross-channel** view: one contact, all their addresses and handles
(email, Signal, …), and their full conversation **history** in one place.

Contacts are created **automatically** from incoming messages — you rarely add one by
hand. For each contact you can:

- edit their **name** and **notes**, and structured **details** (birthday, phone,
  company, role, website, address);
- add or remove **contact methods** (channel + identifier) and **addresses**;
- **merge** two contacts that are really the same person;
- **view in memory** to jump to what the assistant [knows](memory.md) about them.

Deleting a contact keeps the underlying messages.
