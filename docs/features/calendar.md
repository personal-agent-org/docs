# Calendar & agenda

Two related places for "what's coming up": your **Calendar** (real events from connected
calendars) and your **Agenda** (commitments and reminders the assistant tracks for you).

## Calendar

The **Calendar** page (*Kalender*, in the sidebar) merges all your connected **CalDAV**
calendars into one view. Switch between a **Month** (*Monat*) grid and an **Agenda**
(*Agenda*) list, and jump to **Today** (*Heute*).

- Click an event to open it on its **own page** (*Termin*) with full details.
- **New event** (*Neuer Termin*) creates one on any **writable** calendar — title, start
  and end (or all-day), calendar, location and description.
- All-day events are marked accordingly.

No calendar yet? Connect one under **Settings → Integrations** (CalDAV). See
[Integrations](integrations.md).

## Agenda

The **Agenda** page (*Anstehend*, in the sidebar) is where the assistant's
**proactive** side shows up. It brings your **tasks** (badged *Aufgabe*) and your
**upcoming appointments** (*Termine*) together in one list, alongside the commitments and
deadlines it has promised to remind you about.

- When you tell the assistant you'll do something ("I'll get back to Marco about the K8s
  update"), it can **record a commitment** — and it appears here.
- Add your own with a quick line in the input.
- Mark items **Done** (*Erledigt*) or **Dismiss** (*Verwerfen*) them.
- **Hand a task to an agent** (*An Agent übergeben*) — opens a new chat and sets the
  assistant to work on it; the task's **recent runs** are shown so you can follow along.

### Proactive reminders

On a schedule you control, the assistant **reviews what's due** and nudges you in your
[main chat](chat.md#the-main-chat) (and, if enabled, via push notification) — so things
don't slip even if you never open the Agenda. Tune how often under **Settings → Behavior
→ Proactive** (Off / Daily / Every 6 hours / Hourly), and set **quiet hours** so it
doesn't buzz you at night.
